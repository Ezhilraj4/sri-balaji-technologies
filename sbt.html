About the Company

Precision built for industry, trusted worldwide

Sri Balaji Technologies is a leading manufacturer of brass forged ball valves and industrial components, headquartered in Coimbatore — India's engineering capital. We serve both domestic and export markets with consistent quality.

Our product range includes full port 2-piece brass forged ball valves with solid forged ball construction, PTFE teflon seats, and brass spindles — built for water supply, flush valve systems, and chemical plant applications.
